ReFlex VR · Ghost hand biofeedback

Helping people recovering from stroke practise everyday movements with immediate visual guidance.

A healthy “ghost hand” demonstrates each step of a kitchen task. The patient’s hand is recorded and compared stage by stage, with a report for the therapist.
