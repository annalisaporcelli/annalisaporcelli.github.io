Problem: after a stroke, patients repeat exercises without knowing whether the movement is right.

A healthy "ghost hand" demonstrates each step of a kitchen task. The patient's hand is recorded and compared stage by stage, and therapists get a printable report.

Quest 3→Unity recorder→UDP stream→Python analysis→Therapist report

About

Engineer by training, storyteller by habit

I'm finishing a master's in mechatronic engineering at Politecnico di Torino, working on VR rehabilitation for stroke patients. I've built a robot cat, an AR therapy published on Springer, web apps and websites.

I also film and edit, for the European Astronaut Centre, a student satellite team and a university podcast. Knowing how the technology works makes the story clearer.

Unity · C# · Python · PyTorch · OpenCV · ROS · HTML/CSS/JS · Figma · Premiere Pro · After Effects · DaVinci Resolve

Gli strumenti: prima quelli tecnici, poi quelli video. Controlla la lista: alcuni li ho messi io a intuito (PyTorch, OpenCV, ROS).
